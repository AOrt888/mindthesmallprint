About the blog

Mind The Small Print

Zooming in on the details you shouldn't miss.

Why this blog exists

Mind the Small Print breaks down developments in the legal world that I am interested in. I engage largely with cases and issues concerning sport, intellectual property, media & entertainment and commercial law, paying close attention to the details that often decide how things play out.

I started this blog as a way to build my own commercial awareness, deepen my understanding of the law and share my take on the areas I find most interesting. If it helps you catch up on a story you missed or understand something you otherwise wouldn't have, it's doing what it's meant to.

Writer & Editor

Alexei Ortelli

I am an MA Law and BA History graduate from the University of Law and the University of Birmingham respectively. My interests span Sports, IP, Media & Entertainment and Commercial Law, with a particular focus on how small details shape wider legal and commercial outcomes. Mind the Small Print is where I track developments in the areas I care about, unpack the finer points and make sense of emerging issues for myself and others.

If you would like to discuss my work with me or have any questions, feel free to connect with me on LinkedIn or email me at Alexeiortelli@outlook.com.
